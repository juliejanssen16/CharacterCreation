John 3:16

You were made on purpose

Before you had to earn anything, you were already wanted. God's love isn't a reward for good behavior — it's where you started from.

But we all drift

Every one of us pulls away sometimes — hurts others, hides, tries to fill the ache with anything but Him. That gap is real. It's not a life sentence.

So Jesus closed the gap

He didn't wait for you to clean up first. He came, lived, died, and rose again — to carry what was too heavy for you to carry alone.

And now — you're invited

No performance required. No perfect prayer. Just a real yes, however quiet, however small.

Come as you are

If something in you is saying yes — even quietly — that's enough to start. You could pray something like this, in your own words:

"Jesus, thank You for loving me first. I open my heart to You — just as I am. Be near to me."
